Privacy Policy

Itstem TikTok Uploader

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Overview

Itstem TikTok Uploader is a desktop application used to help the operator of Itstem publish authorized video content to TikTok.

2. Information Accessed

When a user connects a TikTok account, the application may access basic account information and permissions necessary to authenticate the account and publish video content through TikTok APIs.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to provide the application's account authentication and video publishing functionality requested by the user.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell TikTok user information or share TikTok user information with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other information used by the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary to operate the application. Users may stop using the application and revoke its TikTok access through their TikTok account settings.

7. Third-Party Services

The application uses TikTok services and APIs. Use of TikTok services is also subject to TikTok's applicable terms and privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

9. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the operator of Itstem TikTok Uploader.
